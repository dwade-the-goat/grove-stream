02 · HOW IT RUNS

Flash, back from the dead

The game plays through Ruffle — an open-source Flash emulator written in Rust/WASM. That means no plugins, no security risk, and it runs full speed on anything from a gaming PC to a school Chromebook.

03 · WHAT YOU GET

Full version

All the official levels and characters, played start to finish. Progress you make in-game saves automatically inside your browser.
